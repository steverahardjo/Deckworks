# Consultant workflow

Use for executive problem solving, strategy recommendations, operating-model
reviews, due diligence, and decision memos presented as slides.

Frame the work as situation → complication → answer. At the narrative gate,
present the issue tree or storyline before building slides, and stop if the
recommendation cannot be separated from its assumptions.

The operating method lives in `skills/consultant.md`; the universal contract and
gate sequence are in `backend/shared/skills/SKILL.md`.
