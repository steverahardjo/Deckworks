# Accounting workflow

Use for financial statements, management reporting, budgets, forecasts,
variance reviews, controls, and audit or close-readout decks.

Start by establishing period, entity, currency, accounting basis, and source
authority. Reconcile totals before drafting conclusions. Show actual versus
plan, prior period, and forecast with explicit units and signs; separate
reported values from estimates and management adjustments.

Prefer a small set of components: `title` for the conclusion, `body` for the
bridge explanation, `chart` for trend or variance, `table` for reconciled
detail, and `callout` for a risk or control exception. Put definitions and
assumptions in speaker notes rather than hiding them in chart labels.

Record formulas, source files, period filters, rounding, and unresolved
reconciliation items in `scratchpad.md`. Stop at the narrative approval gate
if a number cannot be traced to a supplied source. Follow the operational
instructions in `backend/shared/skills/SKILL.md`.
