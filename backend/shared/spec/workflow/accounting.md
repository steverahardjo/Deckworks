# Accounting workflow

Use for financial statements, management reporting, budgets, forecasts,
variance reviews, controls, and audit or close-readout decks.

At setup, establish period, entity, currency, accounting basis, and source
authority. Stop at the narrative gate when a number cannot be traced to a
supplied source, and keep reported values separate from estimates.

The operating method lives in `skills/accounting.md`; the universal contract and
gate sequence are in `backend/shared/skills/SKILL.md`.
