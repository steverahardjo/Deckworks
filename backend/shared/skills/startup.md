# Startup

Use for pitches, product updates, launch narratives, investor updates, and
metric-led operating reviews.

## Narrative method

Lead with the change, the customer problem, or the traction signal. Use short
claims, one memorable metric per slide, and a clear progression from insight to
product or strategy to proof to ask. Treat real metrics, logos, screenshots, and
customer evidence as required assets; do not invent traction.

## Structure

Component choice and layout come from the selected look. Keep speaker notes for
talk track and objections, and record metric definitions and time windows in
`scratchpad.md`.

## Implement

Follow the `create` gate structure, then load the `build` subskill for the
stylesheet, layout, and Python slide-HTML rules.
